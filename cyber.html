What This Desk Believes

Vendor disclosure discipline is degrading industry-wide; the MOVEit shape (silence, then mass exploitation, then a shutdown order with no CVE) will repeat at least twice more before any regulator forces disclosure timelines.

Most attacks called 'sophisticated' in 2026 will turn out to be unpatched edge devices and stolen credentials; sophistication claims are a disclosure strategy, not a technical finding.
