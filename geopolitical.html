What This Desk Believes

Beijing's Taiwan strategy is normalization, not invasion: every exercise exists to make the abnormal routine, and the metric that matters is what stops being reported, not what starts.

Washington's deterrence timeline and Taipei's domestic politics run on different clocks, and the gap widens through 2027; watch budget votes, not communiques.
