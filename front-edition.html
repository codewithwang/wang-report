Binance reopens, and $3.6 billion heads for the exit

Crypto withdrawals test confidence, Hong Kong expands bond collateral use, and Formula One crowns its youngest champion.

Evening briefing

  • CyberBinance reports $3.64 billion in net withdrawals

    Binance reported $3.64 billion in net outflows between 06:00 and 15:30 GMT on 1 November after reopening following a $6.3 billion hack attributed by the FBI to North Korean attackers. The exchange says it has replaced around 95% of stolen assets and will cover all losses; customers are conducting their own confidence survey through the withdrawal button.

  • FinanceMainland bonds gain another job in Hong Kong clearing

    From today, HKCC and SEOCH accept eligible Northbound Bond Connect securities as collateral for cleared derivatives, giving holders another use for existing investments. CMU OmniClear specifies an outright transfer of ownership to the clearing houses, a consequential detail for participants deciding which bonds to deliver.

  • WorldIran warns renewed war could outlast the previous conflict

    Iran's army spokesman warned that another war with the United States could last longer than the previous fighting and that Washington could not dictate its conclusion, AFP reports. The warning followed Donald Trump's statement that further targets remained, leaving diplomatic efforts accompanied by fresh threats of bombardment.

  • CyberNorthflank tells customers to revoke exposed credentials

    Following reported AI-key abuse, Northflank tells all users to assume all environment variables are compromised and rotate all secrets and credentials, including those in workloads, secret groups and global secrets. Revoke existing credentials with issuing providers, generate replacements, update stored secrets, disable public access to add-ons where enabled, and review provider usage and billing logs for unexpected activity.

  • WorldDemocrats approach Tuesday with polling leads and uncertainty

    BBC analysis finds polls favour Democrats taking the US House, with several pivotal Senate contests tied or narrowly leaning their way. Difficulty reaching representative samples and uncertainty over turnout leave room for error, particularly where a few percentage points could decide control.

  • SportsAntonelli becomes Formula One's youngest world champion

    Kimi Antonelli secured the championship at 20 years and 68 days by finishing third in Mexico City behind Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton, becoming Italy's first champion since 1953. With the title requirements comfortably met, third place proved an excellent place to let other people argue about winning.
