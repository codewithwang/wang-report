White’s comeback, SpaceX’s borrowing and a patching deadline

An England retrospective, billions for AI chips and four exploited software flaws.

A short evening edition

  • SportsWhite’s Spain winner caps his England return

    BBC Sport’s new profile traces Ben White’s England comeback through injuries and supporters’ jeers to the goal that proved decisive against Spain on 15 November. Recalled under Thomas Tuchel in March after making himself unavailable under Gareth Southgate, the Arsenal defender has re-established himself at right-back, although he says the full story of his 2022 departure remains untold.

  • Hong KongSpaceX raises billions for chips lenders want on Earth

    Dimsum Daily reports SpaceX sold US$32 billion in bonds on 19 November to fund Nvidia chips through a vehicle leasing them back from the first half of 2027. Citing the Financial Times, it says lenders sought restrictions keeping their collateral on Earth for recovery after default, an unusually literal repossession concern; whether those clauses entered the contracts is unclear, and SpaceX says terrestrial use is intended.

  • CyberFour software flaws join CISA’s exploited catalogue

    The Hacker News reports CISA added flaws in Apache Roller, Cisco Expressway, SAP NetWeaver Enterprise Portal and VMware vCenter to its exploited-vulnerability catalogue on 20 November, with fixes available and a 23 November patch deadline for US Federal Civilian Executive Branch agencies. It links vCenter exploitation to suspected China-linked espionage, while the separately reported Cisco attack remains unattributed.
