Phase III
Rentosertib Phase III IPF trial: Trial opened July 2026 across 47 hospitals in China to test annual FVC decline
n = 320 patients · Does not show yet: Will not measure biological age or aging clocks, only lung function, reads out around 2027 · source · the column (Sep 7)
Study
Insilico aging-clock analysis of GENESIS-IPF serum: Six aging clocks read lower biological age in patients on rentosertib using leftover trial serum
n = 71 patients · Does not show yet: Does not establish rentosertib as an anti-aging therapy, per Insilico's own scientists · source · the column (Sep 7)
Phase II
GENESIS-IPF Phase IIa trial of rentosertib: At 60mg daily, forced vital capacity improved 98.4 mL versus placebo, which fell 20.3 mL
n = 71 patients · Does not show yet: Whether the drug slows aging; only lung function over a year counts toward approval · source · the column (Sep 7)
Phase III
RASolute 302 phase 3 trial of daraxonrasib: Median survival 13.2 months on daraxonrasib versus 6.7 months on chemotherapy, 60 percent reduction in risk of death
n = 500 patients · Does not show yet: Earlier-stage efficacy; lung cancer combo trial still running, frontline pancreatic trial still enrolling · source · the column (Aug 30)
Observational
ProPublica genomic sequencing of Texas-Utah measles outbreak: 12-nucleotide difference across genome links Texas and Utah cases as one unbroken 16-month transmission chain
n = 16 states · Does not show yet: does not itself constitute PAHO's formal elimination-status re-verification, due November · source · the column (Aug 23)
Phase III
Moderna mFlusiva phase 3 trial vs standard-dose flu vaccine: mRNA flu vaccine was 26.6 percent more effective than a licensed standard-dose vaccine, CI 16.7 to 35.4 percent
n = more than 40,000 adults 50 and older · Does not show yet: clinical benefit in adults 65 and older, pending a confirmatory trial · source · the column (Aug 16)

What This Desk Believes

K-pop's industrial model is exporting its labor problems along with its format; the first major unionization story in a Western-market K-pop venture lands within two years.

Award prestige and audience share have fully decoupled in streaming; a trophy is now a B2B marketing spend aimed at talent agencies, not viewers, and the platforms' own spending shows they know it.

Longevity is becoming a regulatory-arbitrage story: the interesting human trials will keep migrating to whichever jurisdiction offers the fastest pathway, and the science will follow the paperwork.

Most consumer biomarker products will fail replication; the measures that survive will be the boring ones (VO2max, grip strength, sleep regularity), and the industry's margins depend on you not noticing that.
