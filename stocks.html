By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSun, Sep 20

Markets held a steady tone today, the major benchmarks trading without sharp dislocation and the rate conversation staying in the background rather than driving the tape. That calm has freed up time for something else: the portfolio's advisors have spent more of this week arguing about positioning than the market itself has actually moved.

The technology sleeve, still the largest and most concentrated part of this book, is where the real activity sits, alongside a leveraged semiconductor position that amplifies every move in the group. Consumer names, the software infrastructure holding, and the broad index and energy exposures are trading in line with their sectors. They are ballast, not decisions, while the harder calls play out in tech.

The headline item is NVIDIA. The advisors have discussed trimming it to a smaller share of the portfolio, selling in three tranches over four to six weeks, with proceeds from the first tranche funding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway's Class B shares. Further out, they have talked about adding health care exposure through UNH or JNJ, layering in international diversification via VXUS, and writing firm rules for the smaller, more volatile names, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines: caps on further buying, exit triggers set in advance rather than decided in the moment. Intel remains under review. The advisors are treating it as a decision to make, not a position to hold indefinitely.
