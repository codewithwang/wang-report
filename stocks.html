Index levels are not in the tape today, so the read starts with the mood. Rates remain the organizing question for anyone holding growth, and a concentrated technology book feels every move in that conversation first. The S&P 500, the Nasdaq and the Hang Seng arrived without figures, so no direction can be reported honestly. Today is a day for reading the stories rather than the screen.

CL

Technology dominates this portfolio, and today's headlines pull in different directions. Apple is reported to have trimmed iPhone reorders as demand cools, while AMD may find a tailwind in a tight supply picture. Software infrastructure had the better day: Okta climbed on AI security enthusiasm and firmer guidance. Consumer cyclical was mixed, with Amazon reshaping its tablet line and Lululemon shoppers drifting toward resale. The broad S&P 500 fund carries a reminder that patience is not always rewarded. A fund called "VOO and Chill" lost money for eight straight years. Chill, it turns out, has a time horizon.

Among individual names, NVIDIA is in talks to acquire Reflection AI, the open-model lab it just backed, while her advisors are discussing a staged trim of the position over several weeks. That first tranche would fund a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway Class B. Soon, the advisors would add healthcare exposure through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, plus an international fund, and write firm rules for SoundHound and Unusual Machines. Intel faces a decision: cut or commit, no limbo. Adobe's profits have soared over a decade, yet the stock trails the market, a rare case of a company outrunning its own share price.
