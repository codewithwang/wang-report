By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSun, Oct 11

The index numbers are not in the feed today. S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng all came through as n/a, so there is no honest read on how the tape is trading, and none will be offered. What can be said is that rates still shape every conversation about this book, from a Hong Kong government iBond that anchors the fixed income side to a stack of growth names that care a great deal about where yields settle. A tape with no numbers has no opinion, which makes it the most agreeable thing on the desk today.

The portfolio is a concentrated technology book with a few outposts. Technology dominates: Apple, NVIDIA, Adobe, AMD, Intel, CrowdStrike, Palantir, SoundHound AI, Unusual Machines and Micron, plus a leveraged semiconductor fund that magnifies the same bet. Around it sit Amazon and Lululemon in consumer cyclical, Okta in software infrastructure, a broad S&P 500 fund, an energy fund, and the iBond. Sector moves were not supplied today, so the exposure is what can be reported, not the direction.

In the names, Okta reached a record high even as growth slows to 11 percent. Apple is betting on a foldable iPhone, Nvidia is in talks to buy Reflection AI, and AMD is raising GDDR6 prices for partners. Her advisors want the Nvidia stake trimmed in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first tranche funding a Berkshire Hathaway Class B position. Next, they would add healthcare through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, plus an international fund, and write hard rules for SoundHound and Unusual Machines. They also want Intel resolved, cut or commit, with no limbo. Currency and estate tax reviews are on the list too.
