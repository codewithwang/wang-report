By Charmaine LoNews AnchorFri, Oct 2

Index levels for the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng did not come through this morning, so there is no honest number to quote. The headlines do show buyers still paying up for artificial intelligence. Cybersecurity names hit one-year highs on the theme, and HPE reached a record after a large AMD deal. VOO, meanwhile, flashed a warning signal for tech-heavy portfolios, a notice that arrived after the party was already catered.

In the portfolio's technology book, Okta and CrowdStrike are at yearly highs with the cyber rally, Adobe is moving as traders hunt for a catalyst, and AMD is lifted by the HPE deal. Apple faces a multibillion dollar verdict over iPhone and Watch haptics. In consumer cyclical, Lululemon is climbing, and Amazon is reportedly shopping Nvidia chips to investors, then leasing them back. Amazon is selling the chips to rent them. The index fund, the energy fund and the iBond offered no headline today.

On the names, her advisors are focused on NVIDIA, the largest concentration in a technology-heavy book. They have discussed trimming it in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first funding a new Berkshire Hathaway Class B position. Soon after, they would add healthcare through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, plus international exposure through VXUS. For SoundHound and Unusual Machines, they favor hard rules: no adds, half off on a double, full exit if the thesis breaks. On Intel, they want a decision, cut or commit, with no limbo. A currency review and a US estate tax review are also on the list.
