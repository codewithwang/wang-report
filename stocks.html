Markets were closed for data today. No print on the S&P 500, the Nasdaq, or the Hang Seng, which left the desk reading positioning and commentary rather than price action. Quieter session than the one before it. Not a quieter story.

The portfolio's concentration is still technology, and technology is still where the conversation is. Chips, software infrastructure, and platform computing continue to set the tone for the book's daily swings. The index and energy sleeves sit as the steadier counterweight. The Hong Kong Government iBond holds its place as the calm end of the shelf.

NVIDIA, the largest position in the book, is the center of today's discussion. The advisors have laid out a plan to trim the stake in three tranches over four to six weeks, using the first tranche to fund a new position in Berkshire Hathaway Class B, a stake they describe as modest and deliberate.

Beyond that, the advisors are weighing a healthcare allocation between UnitedHealth and Johnson & Johnson, and discussing international exposure through Vanguard's total international fund. Intel is flagged for a decision: cut or commit. SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines get firm rules instead of discussion: no further buying, half off on any double, full exit if the thesis breaks.

A foreign exchange review is on the list, a hedge against pressure on the Hong Kong dollar peg, alongside a look at estate tax exposure for a non-US person.

Discipline, not drama, is the word from the desk today. On a day with no tape to argue with, that's the easiest promise in the world to keep.
