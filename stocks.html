By Charmaine LoNews AnchorThu, Sep 10

Markets held a quiet posture through the session. Index data was still settling, traders showed no urgency in either direction, and rate expectations remained the dominant conversation on every desk. The mood was patient rather than convicted, a tape waiting for its next data point rather than making one.

Technology carried the portfolio's center of gravity today. Chip and AI-infrastructure names moved in step, and the leveraged semiconductor exposure amplified whatever direction that group took. Consumer names traded on their own logic, tied more to spending signals than to the broader tape. Energy and index exposures played their usual role as ballast against the concentrated technology book. Fixed income anchored the portfolio's quieter corner.The advisors have been talking about NVIDIA, specifically about trimming it down from its position as the portfolio's largest holding. That will happen in stages over the coming weeks rather than all at once, with the proceeds seeding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. Healthcare and international diversification are next on the list, to be phased in gradually. On the smaller, more volatile names, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, the advisors want firm rules written now. Write the rule before the next swing writes it for you. Intel remains a name the desk says it needs to stop deliberating and start deciding on.
