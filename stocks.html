From the desk · Daily read
By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSun, Sep 27

Sector momentum keeps pulling this book in two directions at once. Fixed income sits quiet while the iBond does its one job, and the technology names are still doing the heavy lifting for the portfolio, Apple, NVIDIA, Adobe, AMD, Intel, CrowdStrike and Palantir carrying the bulk of it, with Micron and the smaller names, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, adding volatility more than ballast. Software infrastructure through Okta and consumer names Amazon and Lululemon are riding the broader tape rather than setting it, and the leveraged semiconductor sleeve amplifies whatever the chip names decide to do that day, in either direction.

Concentration risk has met its own success. NVIDIA has grown into the largest position almost by accident of performance, and that is the good kind of headache to have.

Her advisors have been direct about it. The plan on the table now is to trim NVIDIA down to a smaller share of the book, selling in three tranches over four to six weeks rather than all at once, with proceeds from the first tranche seeding a new position in Berkshire Hathaway Class B, a name that had been sitting on the watchlist. Next in line, should conviction hold, is a modest healthcare allocation through UNH or JNJ and some international diversification via VXUS, both phased in over similar timeframes. On the smaller, more volatile names, SOUN and UMAC, the advisors want hard rules written down now: no further adds, half off on a double, full exit if the thesis breaks. Discipline replaces debate later. Intel remains a decision deferred, evaluate and commit or cut, no more sitting in between, while an FX review of HKD-linked exposure and a US estate tax review round out the watch list.
