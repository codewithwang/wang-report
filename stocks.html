By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSat, Sep 12

Trading desks entered the session with limited fresh macro data on the tape, leaving the major benchmarks without a clean print to react to. Rate expectations are doing the talking, and the market's mood is cautious patience rather than conviction in either direction. Absent a clear signal, positioning discipline matters more than prediction.

Inside this book, technology remains the dominant force, spanning chipmakers, software security names and a leveraged semiconductor vehicle that amplifies whatever the sector does, for better or worse. The cyclical consumer names and the software infrastructure holding move on their own logic, while the index and energy funds provide the ballast underneath a portfolio that leans hard into one theme. A three times leveraged semiconductor fund is not a hedge. It is a statement of confidence, and gravity does not care which way that confidence points.

Among individual names, NVIDIA sits at the center of attention. The advisors have discussed trimming it down from its outsized place in the portfolio, executing that reduction in stages over the coming weeks rather than all at once. Proceeds from the first stage are expected to help fund a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. Looking further out, the advisors have flagged healthcare and international diversification as priorities, along with firmer rules for the portfolio's more speculative small names. A decision on Intel is still pending.
