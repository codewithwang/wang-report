The tape arrives without index levels today, so the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng go unquoted here rather than guessed at, and there is no fresh read on rates. The mood comes from the headlines. An AI rally lifted cyber stocks, with Okta touching a one-year high, while a warning from Nike rattled the apparel group. Risk appetite is alive in software and cautious in sneakers.

For the portfolio, the sectors split along that line. The concentrated technology book has the better of it. SpaceXAI is set to switch on 420,000 NVIDIA GPUs in November, and AMD landed an $8.2 billion deal that caught Wall Street's eye. Okta carries the software infrastructure flag. Consumer cyclical is the softer corner, where Lululemon sits among the names caught in the Nike warning and Apple faces a $5.7 billion verdict over iPhone and Watch haptics. The fixed income, energy and index fund holdings had no headline of their own, which for a bond is the best kind of news day.

Her advisors want to trim NVIDIA in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first tranche funding a new Berkshire Hathaway Class B position. Next in line are a healthcare name, either UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, and an international fund. SoundHound and Unusual Machines get hard rules in writing: no adds, half off on a double, full exit if the thesis breaks. On Intel, the advice is to cut or commit, with no limbo. A review of currency exposure and US estate tax is also on the list. Nobody has proposed a committee.
