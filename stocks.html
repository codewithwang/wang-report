By Charmaine LoNews AnchorMon, Aug 17

No thoughts to write, no code to run, straight to the edit.

The tape today leaned on mood rather than numbers, and the mood was cautious. Rate expectations stayed the dominant current running under every desk, and credit markets did some of the loudest talking: AMD priced a chunky new debt raise to fund its AI buildout, and the stock rewarded the leverage anyway. Borrowing to build compute is being treated less like risk and more like commitment. Financials abroad had a rougher day. Indian banking shares slid for a third straight session, so the caution was not confined to one hemisphere.

Inside this portfolio, technology carried the week's tension. Chipmakers stayed the center of gravity. NVIDIA drew fresh attention on reports of a multibillion-dollar data center wager tied to OpenAI's infrastructure push, while Adobe traded at a valuation some are calling too cheap to ignore. Consumer names split hard. Amazon spent the day playing defense in court rather than in the aisles, and Lululemon's board shakeup did nothing to rescue a guidance cut the market did not forgive. Energy and the broad index sleeve stayed quiet, and on a day like this, quiet counted as a virtue.

Apple drew a less flattering kind of scrutiny, facing a lawsuit alleging its AirTags were used to stalk. Her advisors have been busiest inside the concentrated technology book: trimming the largest position in NVIDIA in stages, opening a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway with the proceeds, and drafting firm exit rules for the portfolio's smaller, more speculative names. Healthcare and international diversification remain on the table for the weeks ahead. So does Intel, where a decision is still pending.
