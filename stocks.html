By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSun, Sep 13

Wall Street's macro backdrop offered no clean read today, with the major benchmarks giving neither the rate mood nor the broader economy a story to react to. The portfolio's own housekeeping did the talking instead.

The technology sleeve, still the dominant force in this book, is where the actual movement is. Semiconductor and AI-adjacent names from NVIDIA and AMD to Micron and Palantir continue to set the tone for a concentrated technology book, while the leveraged semiconductor exposure and the software infrastructure position in Okta round out a portfolio leaning hard into the chip cycle. The consumer cyclical and energy sleeves sit quieter by comparison, ballast rather than headline.

The name in focus is NVIDIA. The advisors have discussed trimming the largest position down to a smaller share of the book, done in three tranches over four to six weeks rather than all at once. The first tranche, the advisors note, would fund a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. Beyond that, the conversation turns to diversification: a healthcare allocation, an international fund, and firmer discipline around the more speculative names, where the advisors want hard exit rules written down before the next swing, not after it. Intel remains a decision deferred. The advisors have flagged it plainly: cut or commit.
