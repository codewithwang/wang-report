The index screens did not report this edition. S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng readings were unavailable at filing, and there was no fresh word on rates or the economy, so the tape stays unread and nothing here pretends otherwise. The portfolio itself is a concentrated technology book, with a Hong Kong government bond, a broad U.S. index fund and an energy fund doing the quieter work around it.

Technology is where the news is, and it does not move as one. Apple slid on reports of deep iPhone 18 production cuts. Okta, the portfolio's software infrastructure name, hit record highs, though the headline concedes the AI payoff is two years away. Adobe is contending with an AI tool that cloned seven of its biggest apps and released them as open source. In semiconductors, ARK trimmed its chip bets, with AMD among the sales. Amazon, the consumer cyclical anchor, is retiring its Fire tablets in favour of Alexa slates, which is one way to describe a product line ending.

NVIDIA is reportedly eyeing a $25 billion Reflection AI deal that could become a full acquisition. Her advisors, meanwhile, want the largest position trimmed in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first tranche funding a new Berkshire Hathaway Class B stake. Healthcare comes next, through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, then international exposure through VXUS. SoundHound and Unusual Machines get hard rules. Intel gets a decision: cut it or commit to it, with no limbo. An FX review and a U.S. estate tax check are also under consideration.
