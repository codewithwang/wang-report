By Charmaine LoNews AnchorFri, Jul 31

Amazon's twelve percent surge on AI-winner status did the heavy lifting for the tape today, even as the memory chip warning out of Cupertino, delivered on the outgoing chief executive's final earnings call as a "hundred year flood," reminded the desk that this rally has more than one storyline running. Semiconductor names traded on both fear and relief in the same session: chip makers surged, chip buyers flinched, and NVIDIA's slide below the 190 mark drew at least one call for a rebound as steep as the drop.

The portfolio's technology book, its largest and most concentrated position, carries the day's crosscurrents most visibly. Chipmakers advanced while chip pricing turned into a warning label, software security absorbed an acquisition and a zero-day in the same week, and consumer names split cleanly, Amazon's AI halo lifting one and a prominent short seller's new stake lifting the other for entirely different reasons. Diversification by circumstance, not design.

Her advisors have been busiest around the technology book itself. Guidance calls for trimming NVIDIA down to a smaller share of the portfolio in stages over the coming weeks, with early proceeds seeding a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. Behind that, the desk is weighing healthcare and international additions to balance the book, drafting firm exit rules for the portfolio's smaller, speculative names, and pressing for a clean decision on Intel rather than an indefinite hold. A currency hedge and an estate tax review round out the list, filed for now under consider.
