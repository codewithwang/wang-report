Three tranches over a month, for the single biggest position in the account. Call it patience, or call it a hedge against being wrong at speed.

The sector story is concentration. Technology dominates this book, from Apple and Adobe through AMD, Micron, CrowdStrike and Palantir, with a leveraged semiconductor fund adding torque on top of an already crowded chip exposure. Healthcare, through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, and international exposure via VXUS are both under active discussion as additions in the coming weeks. SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines get formal guardrails: no further adds, trim on strength, exit on any break in thesis. Intel is a decision the advisors still owe the book.
