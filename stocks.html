By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSat, Sep 26

Markets closed flat today. No clean read on direction from the index tape, and the rate conversation stayed where it has been: watching, not moving. That backdrop is why the portfolio's advisors have been trimming and rebalancing now, in the calm, rather than waiting for a reason to.

The book's center of gravity is still technology, anchored by Apple, NVIDIA, Adobe and Micron, with software infrastructure and consumer cyclical names filling out the rest alongside a fixed income sleeve in HK Government iBonds. Leveraged semiconductor exposure and broad index and energy funds round out a portfolio that leans hard into where the growth story lives, and just as hard into the volatility that comes with it.

The headline move under discussion: trimming NVIDIA down from the largest position in the book, staged across several tranches over the coming weeks rather than sold in one motion, with proceeds seeding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. The advisors have also flagged healthcare and international exposure as additions worth building out gradually. They've proposed hard exit rules for the portfolio's more speculative names, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, rules written down before they're needed rather than after.

Intel remains an open question the advisors want settled one way or the other. A currency hedge tied to the Hong Kong dollar peg is under review.

Committees, as always, can meet about it later.
