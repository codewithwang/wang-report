By Charmaine LoNews AnchorWed, Oct 7

The macro tape is thin today. Index levels for the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng did not reach the desk, so there is no honest reading on how they are trading, and nothing on the economy or the rate mood either. The day's tone came from individual stories instead.

In the sectors this portfolio holds, the split was sharp. Consumer cyclical had the worst of it: Lululemon fell 17 percent on a growth warning, and a securities fraud probe opened the same day. Amazon had the better news, pledging $1 billion to towns that host its data centers. In software, Okta reached three-year highs. Technology was mixed. AMD's chief spoke of a supply chain push worth tens of billions as AI demand outruns output, Apple is building a smart home line with LG, and Adobe drew the question of how much further it can fall. The Vanguard S&P 500 ETF was described as quietly an AI fund, which is a modest job title for an index.

Among the names in focus, NVIDIA drew attention as SpaceX seeks $40 billion in Apollo-led financing to buy its chips. Her advisors have discussed trimming the NVIDIA stake in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first tranche funding a new Berkshire Hathaway Class B position. They also point to healthcare and international additions, firm exit rules for SoundHound and Unusual Machines, and a cut-or-commit decision on Intel.
