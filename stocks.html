By Charmaine LoNews AnchorTue, Sep 8

Wall Street's rate mood stayed cautious into the session. The major indices held a narrow, watchful range as traders parsed the same signals they parsed yesterday and found no new answers. The tape is not panicking. It is not celebrating either. It is waiting for someone else to move first.

The portfolio's sector mix reflects that tension directly. A concentrated technology book carries the load, spanning chipmakers, software infrastructure, and AI-adjacent names, while a leveraged semiconductor position amplifies whatever the chip complex decides to do next. Consumer names and the fixed income sleeve sit quieter by comparison. The energy exposure offers the closest thing to a hedge against a macro mood that could still turn. The result is a book built for conviction in one direction, which is a fine strategy right up until the direction changes.

Among individual names, the advisors have turned their attention to NVIDIA, discussing a trim toward a smaller share of the technology book, executed gradually rather than all at once. Proceeds under discussion would help fund a new position in Berkshire Hathaway, a rotation toward ballast. Also on the table: adding healthcare and international exposure over the coming weeks, writing firmer rules around the portfolio's smaller speculative names, and settling the Intel question one way or the other. The advisors have discussed it. The portfolio hasn't decided.
