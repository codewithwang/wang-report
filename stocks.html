Technology, the dominant sleeve of this concentrated book, is carrying most of the weather. In semiconductors, Nvidia is rising despite the jitters, while AMD is courting Samsung Foundry to diversify its manufacturing. On names, the advisors have been specific. Nvidia is to be trimmed in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first funding a new Berkshire Hathaway Class B position. Soon after, they would add healthcare through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, and international exposure through VXUS. SoundHound and Unusual Machines get written rules: no adds, and a full exit if the thesis breaks. Intel must be cut or committed to, with no limbo, a firm policy for a stock that has made limbo a lifestyle. The advisors are also weighing an FX review and a US estate tax review.
