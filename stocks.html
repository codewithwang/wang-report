The index screens are blank on the tape today. The S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng all came through without a print, so there is no direction to report and none will be invented. The rate mood and the broader economy are likewise absent from the data in hand. A quiet tape, or at least an unreadable one.

By sector, the book leans heavily on technology, with a few counterweights. Technology carries the most names, from Apple and NVIDIA to Palantir, Micron and the smaller speculative plays, and a leveraged semiconductor fund sits on top of that exposure. Okta covers software infrastructure, Amazon and Lululemon cover consumer cyclical, and the defensive side is the HK Government iBond, a Vanguard S&P 500 fund and a Vanguard energy fund. No sector moves are available today, so what can be said is how concentrated the book is, not which way it went.

On the names, Okta hit a record high on an AI payday still two years out, which is optimism on long invoice terms. Amazon reshuffled Alexa leadership. Nvidia is eyeing a takeover of Reflection AI, a lab it just backed, so it is now shopping at its own investments. Her advisors want the NVIDIA position trimmed in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first tranche funding a Berkshire Hathaway Class B stake. Next come healthcare through UNH or JNJ, an international fund, and hard exit rules for SoundHound and Unusual Machines. Intel faces a decision: cut or commit, no limbo. They are also weighing an FX review and a US estate tax check.
