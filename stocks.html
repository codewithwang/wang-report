By Charmaine LoNews AnchorThu, Aug 27

NVIDIA has bought Hugging Face for nearly thirteen billion dollars. Amazon has tripled its chip order to feed the same appetite. The market's read on both today was mixed, with the major indices offering no clean verdict on whether the AI trade is maturing or just getting louder. The mood this morning has less to do with rates and more to do with which companies are converting the AI story into revenue, and which are riding the headline.

Inside this book, that split shows up sector by sector. Technology, where the bulk of this portfolio lives, is pulling two ways at once. Okta jumped on fresh identity security demand tied to AI threats. Adobe slid with the rest of software on fears that AI erodes its own product moat. Consumer names split too, Amazon lifted by its chip order, Lululemon facing questions about whether its 2026 outlook survives contact with reality. The leveraged semiconductor position and the broad index fund are, for now, just along for the ride.

Her advisors have been circling the portfolio's concentration risk this week, discussing a staged trim of the largest position, NVIDIA, sold down in tranches over the coming weeks, with proceeds seeding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. They have also flagged Intel for a decision rather than a shrug, and floated healthcare and international allocations to balance a concentrated technology book. Cathie Wood's ARK has already made its own version of that call, trimming AMD for Broadcom.
