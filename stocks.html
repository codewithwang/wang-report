Technology and semiconductors show the clearest pressure in the available headlines. Neither establishes how the broader sector is trading now. Among consumer holdings, Amazon faces spending questions; no update is supplied for Lululemon. Current moves are also unavailable for energy, the S&P 500 fund and the Hong Kong government iBond.

The advisors propose trimming NVIDIA in stages over four to six weeks, with the first sale funding a new Berkshire Hathaway position. Their broader discussion favours healthcare and international diversification. It also calls for firm trading rules for SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, a clear decision on Intel, and reviews of currency exposure and US estate tax. These are proposals. Berkshire Hathaway has a place in the discussion; a place in the portfolio depends on the proposed NVIDIA sale.
