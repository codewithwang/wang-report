From the desk · Daily read
By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSun, Sep 6

Markets closed flat today, or the tape gave nothing away: the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng all sat where they were, which is its own kind of statement in a week when rate mood has been the only mover. Traders spent the session parsing the same Fed commentary they parsed yesterday and reached the same conclusion: no one wants to blink first before the next print. Call it a market waiting for a headline nobody has written yet.

Inside the book, the story is concentration. The technology sleeve, still the largest position by a wide margin across Apple, Nvidia, Adobe, AMD, Intel, CrowdStrike, Palantir, Micron, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, sets the portfolio's mood for the day, and the leveraged semiconductor exposure amplifies whatever that mood happens to be. Amazon and Lululemon, plus the software infrastructure position in Okta, trade more on their own news than the macro tape. The index and energy funds, and the HK Government iBond, sit quietly doing what ballast is supposed to do.

The advisors have been busiest around Nvidia, discussing a trim toward a smaller share of the position, staged across three tranches over the coming month and change, with proceeds earmarked to open a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. Also on the table: building out healthcare exposure through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, adding international diversification via VXUS, and writing strict discipline rules around SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines before either name gets another dollar. Intel remains a decision deferred, which the advisors have noted is itself a decision.
