By Charmaine LoNews AnchorMon, Oct 5

The index data is not in hand this morning. The S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng readings all came through as unavailable, so there is no tape to read. The headlines offer a backdrop: agentic AI fears are weighing on parts of the consumer and software complex, and a group of athletic apparel names fell together. That is a mood, not a level, and it will have to do until the screens come back.

In this portfolio, technology dominates: a concentrated book across chips, security, software and a few speculative small caps, and the news there is loud. Nvidia debuted Rubin with a promise of up to a tenfold cut in AI costs. AMD reached a one trillion dollar market cap with Nvidia's position in its sights. Okta's identity ecosystem passed 25 partners as AI gateways become the new enforcement layer. Consumer cyclical is under pressure, with Amazon lagging on agentic AI worries and Lululemon sliding alongside Nike, Adidas and Under Armour. The index fund and energy ETF produced no headlines of their own.

On the names, the advisors are focused on Nvidia. They have discussed trimming it in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first tranche funding a new Berkshire Hathaway Class B position. Next, they want healthcare exposure through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, plus an international fund. SoundHound and Unusual Machines get firm rules: no adds, half off on a double, full exit if the thesis breaks. Intel gets a choice, cut or committed to, with no limbo, and it is the only holding the advisors have put on a deadline. A currency review and an estate tax check round out the list. Adobe's buyback pile sits quietly in the background.
