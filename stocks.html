Adobe, by contrast, spent the day adding AI-generated music and sound effects to Firefly, which is either innovation or a warning, depending on how you feel about royalty-free saxophone.

Technology told two stories at once today: pressure on the chipmakers, quiet momentum in software. The identity-security trade still has believers even when the broader tape gets choppy. Amazon's drone ambitions read as offense. Lululemon lost its third senior executive in a management exodus that no one at the company is calling a coincidence anymore.

The advisors have been busy behind the scenes on this book. They are trimming the largest position, Nvidia, in stages over the coming weeks, and redirecting part of the proceeds into a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. Healthcare and international exposure are next on the list, alongside firmer discipline on the portfolio's more speculative names, where the rule is now simple: no adds, and an exit if the thesis breaks. Intel sits on notice too, the advisors say, with a decision expected rather than a drift.
