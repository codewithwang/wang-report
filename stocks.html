By Charmaine LoNews AnchorMon, Aug 3

Portfolio positioning ripples through the tape today, with the Nasdaq's tech-heavy composition mirroring the fund's own concentrated technology book. Apple shed 426 billion dollars in market value over two sessions. Intel had a similar Monday. NVIDIA, meanwhile, is doing something quieter: sitting still, stuck near the same level for weeks, waiting on a catalyst that Big Tech earnings so far haven't supplied.

Software is having a better month than the chipmakers. Adobe and its peers are staging a sharp July rebound, and cybersecurity names are catching a separate tailwind, with CrowdStrike and Palo Alto rallying on fears that AI agents make tempting new hacking targets. An industry selling protection gets a lift whenever the reasons to fear for it multiply.

The advisors have been busy. They're trimming the NVIDIA position down to a smaller share of the book, doing it in three tranches over the next several weeks rather than all at once, with proceeds from the first tranche funding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. Waiting in the wings: a healthcare allocation built around UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, and international exposure through a broad ex-US fund.

Intel gets no such patience. The advisors want a decision there, not a holding pattern. They're drafting hard exit rules for the portfolio's more speculative names before enthusiasm outruns discipline again.
