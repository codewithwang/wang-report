By Charmaine LoNews AnchorMon, Aug 31

Georgia handed federal prosecutors a tidy case this week, two women accused of a ten million dollar Amazon fraud scheme. Platform trust is a line item too, even when the tape is busy elsewhere. The broader market spent the day confirming what the index has been quietly admitting for months, that record highs increasingly describe two companies rather than five hundred, and Friday's reversal in chip names showed how quickly sentiment turns when the Federal Reserve so much as clears its throat.

Inside the portfolio, software and semiconductors told opposite stories under the same roof. Okta jumped sharply after lifting its 2027 outlook on demand for AI agent security, a rare bright spot in an infrastructure category otherwise having a rough month. Adobe touched a fifty two week low as software broadly got sold. Semiconductors were more mixed than the index headlines suggest. AMD picked up a wave of analyst upgrades even as NVIDIA absorbed the Friday reversal tied to rate anxiety, leaving the portfolio's leveraged semiconductor exposure the most volatile line on the sheet. Consumer names split as well, Amazon dealing with the fraud headline and Lululemon falling on brand concerns greeting its incoming chief executive, while Apple's leadership transition drew a public promise that artificial intelligence comes first.

The advisors have been the busiest voice in the room. They are trimming NVIDIA down from its outsized share in careful stages over the coming weeks, using the early proceeds to open a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. Beyond that they are eyeing healthcare and international diversification, weighing hard exit rules on the portfolio's smaller speculative names, and pressing for a decision on Intel one way or the other. Indecision, they note, is itself a position. It just doesn't show up on the sheet.
