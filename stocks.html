Amazon led the tape higher after lifting its cloud investment plans, though the broader market stayed mixed as chip names sold off on Fed dissent chatter. The portfolio's concentrated technology book had a rough session. Consumer names split: Amazon's cloud strength lifted the stock while Lululemon faces a fresh lawsuit alongside Nike over discount claims. Okta was the software desk's mover, announcing an acquisition of an AI security startup.

Apple posted record revenue. The market shrugged and sent shares lower anyway, a reminder that Wall Street grades on a curve nobody sees in advance. Her advisors have been trimming the NVIDIA position in stages over the coming weeks, redirecting part of the proceeds to open a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. Talk has turned to building out healthcare and international exposure, setting firmer rules around the portfolio's smaller speculative names, and deciding, one way or the other, what to do with Intel.
