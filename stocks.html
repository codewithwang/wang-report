From the desk · Daily read
By Charmaine LoNews AnchorThu, Sep 17

Markets took the day off across the board, S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Hang Seng all sitting out the headline count, which left the portfolio's own positioning to carry the story. Rates stayed the quiet variable underneath everything. With no index prints to react to, the day was less about what the market did and more about what the advisors chose to do with the quiet.

Technology remains the dominant force in this book, still concentrated, still carrying most of its character: software infrastructure, the leveraged semiconductor exposure, and the broad index and energy funds round out the rest. It is a portfolio built for a moving market. Today it did not move.

NVIDIA is the name in focus. The advisors have discussed trimming its outsized share, selling in three tranches over four to six weeks rather than all at once, with proceeds from the first tranche earmarked to open a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. They have also flagged adding healthcare exposure through UNH or JNJ and building out international diversification via VXUS, both gradually over the coming weeks. Separately, they have discussed writing firm rules for the smaller, more volatile names, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, and making a final call on Intel rather than continuing to hold it in limbo.

Call it homework day. The market gave the advisors nothing to trade against, so they traded against their own portfolio instead.
