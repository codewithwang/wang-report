By Charmaine LoNews AnchorFri, Sep 25

Markets held flat today. The S&P 500, the Nasdaq, and the Hang Seng all closed without a print worth quoting, which is its own kind of statement. Rate mood stayed the current running underneath every desk, with the market still parsing what comes next rather than reacting to what already happened. In that kind of tape, positioning mattered more than headlines, and today was a day for positioning.

The portfolio's center of gravity is technology, and that book set the tone. Semiconductor and AI-adjacent names did most of the talking, while the leveraged semiconductor exposure amplified whatever mood the chip complex was in. Consumer cyclical held its usual balance between the software giant and the athletic retailer. Fixed income sat quietly in the iBond doing what fixed income is paid to do, and the broad index and energy sleeves rounded out the ballast. Software infrastructure, represented by the identity name, traded as a single quiet line in a portfolio that otherwise likes to make noise.

The advisors have been talking about NVIDIA. The conversation is about trimming, not abandoning: a concentrated position eased down in stages over the coming weeks, with part of the proceeds earmarked to open a new Berkshire Hathaway stake. Healthcare and international diversification are next on the list, alongside a promise, the advisors say, to finally write hard rules for the portfolio's most speculative names instead of waiting for the market to write them first.

Intel remains the one position everyone agrees needs a decision. Nobody has set a date.
