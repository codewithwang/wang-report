From the desk · Daily read
By Charmaine LoNews AnchorFri, Sep 25

Markets closed flat today. The S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Hang Seng all traded without a clear directional print, leaving rate expectations largely where they sat yesterday. The tape gave nothing to react to, so positioning discipline mattered more than conviction, and that is where this portfolio's attention turned.

The technology book remains the dominant force in this portfolio, still concentrated and still carrying the bulk of its risk, alongside a leveraged semiconductor sleeve that amplifies whatever the chip complex does next. Consumer cyclical names and the fixed income sleeve, anchored by the HK Government iBond, sit quieter by comparison. Energy and the broad index exposures continue to serve as ballast against a technology-heavy core.

NVIDIA is the name in focus today. The advisors have discussed trimming it to a smaller share of the book, executed in three tranches over four to six weeks rather than all at once. Proceeds from that first tranche are earmarked to open a new position in Berkshire Hathaway Class B, a diversification move the advisors have been weighing.

Beyond that, the advisors have flagged a healthcare allocation, likely UNH or JNJ, and an international sleeve via VXUS. They have also set firmer exit rules on the portfolio's more speculative names, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines: no adds, trim on strength, out entirely if the thesis breaks. Intel remains a name the advisors say needs a decision, not more patience.
