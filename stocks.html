By Charmaine LoNews AnchorTue, Aug 4

Amazon's push past three trillion dollars in market value carried the tape today, with the major indices firm and the rate conversation quiet enough that traders spent more time on earnings than on the Fed. Semiconductors stayed the loudest sector in the room. AMD headed into its own earnings report running hot, and NVIDIA kept climbing even as a fresh round of copyright litigation from rights holders gave some investors pause. The stock and its legal exposure rose together.

Technology carried the week's weight while software infrastructure and consumer cyclical both had something to point to. Amazon's milestone lifted the cyclical sleeve. Okta's acquisition of Permiso, aimed at identity threat detection, gave the infrastructure book a strategic story rather than just a price move. Adobe touched a two month high after regulators cleared its Topaz Labs deal. The broad market sleeve is closing in on a trillion dollars in tracked assets, the index fund doing its job quietly while the individual names do the talking.

Apple spent the day in a different kind of fight, suing the UK government over demands to access encrypted user data. That one is about principle, not earnings.

The advisors have been busy closer to home. They are trimming the largest technology position down to a smaller share of the book, in stages over the coming weeks, and opening a new position funded from that trim. A healthcare allocation and an international fund add are next, along with firm rules for the portfolio's smaller speculative names. Intel remains the outlier: the advisors say it needs a decision, not more patience. Even Intel would probably agree.
