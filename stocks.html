By Charmaine LoNews AnchorMon, Sep 28

Markets closed flat to little changed today, no clear directional signal from the major benchmarks. The S&P and Nasdaq held steady alongside the Hang Seng, leaving traders with a session more notable for what it did not do than what it did. Rate expectations stayed the quiet backdrop to all of it, the number everyone quotes and nobody can pin down.Inside this portfolio, the split is a familiar one. The technology book, still the largest position by a wide margin, spans chipmakers, software security, and AI infrastructure, alongside a leveraged semiconductor bet that amplifies whatever the sector does next. Consumer names and a broad index holding round out the growth side, while an energy fund and a Hong Kong government savings bond sit on the other end, doing the unglamorous work of ballast. A concentrated technology book invites concentrated risk, and today nothing in the tape forced anyone's hand.

Her advisors have been busier than the market. Guidance calls for trimming NVIDIA gradually, in tranches over the coming weeks, with proceeds seeding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway, a rotation from momentum toward patience. Healthcare and international diversification are under discussion for the months ahead, alongside firm exit rules for the portfolio's more speculative names. Intel, meanwhile, has been told plainly: commit or cut. No more waiting around.
