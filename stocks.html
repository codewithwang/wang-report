By Charmaine LoNews AnchorFri, Sep 4

Nvidia announced a nearly thirteen billion dollar acquisition of Hugging Face today, and the semiconductor trade moved the way it usually does when Nvidia sneezes: AMD and Intel both felt it, a warning shot fired across the sector rather than a rising tide. Okta jumped after hours on a revenue forecast that beat expectations cleanly. Adobe changed chief executives after a fifteen-year run at the top. Lululemon did not have as kind a day, falling to an eight-year low after its third guidance cut. Money rotated out of growth and into gold and Treasurys, even as the big index funds kept pulling in flows.

That rotation matters for a portfolio built heavily around technology and semiconductors, including a leveraged bet on the chip trade, alongside a fixed income anchor in HK Government iBonds and consumer names now led by Amazon after Lululemon's stumble. Apple also picked up a two point seven billion pound lawsuit in the UK over app tracking practices, one more line item in a year already full of them. The technology book remains the largest position by a wide margin. Today's Nvidia and Adobe headlines sit right on top of that concentration.

Her advisors have been discussing trimming Nvidia down to a smaller share of the book, selling in stages over the coming weeks, with proceeds seeding a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. They have also been talking healthcare and international diversification as later additions, firm rules for the smaller speculative names SoundHound and Unusual Machines, a decision point on Intel, and, further down the list, currency hedging and estate planning considerations. None of it executed today. All of it on the desk.
