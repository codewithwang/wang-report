The macro tape is blank this morning. Index levels for the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng weren't available at filing time, so there is no direction to report and no read on rates. The day's technology news was soft, which matters for a portfolio built heavily around that sector.

The sectors on the book are not moving together. Technology, by far the largest exposure, is under pressure. The semiconductor names are weak, and the leveraged semiconductor fund will feel it more than anything else held. Consumer cyclical is dealing with restructuring at both of its names. Software infrastructure is digesting a big run. The index fund, the energy fund and the Hong Kong government iBond are the steadier ballast in an otherwise tech-led book.

NVIDIA is the name to watch. Jensen Huang's fortune slipped below $200 billion as tech stocks sank, and the advisors have discussed trimming the position in three tranches over four to six weeks, with the first tranche funding a new Berkshire Hathaway Class B stake. Intel slid on reports of Musk's Terafab talks with TSMC, and the advisors want a decision on it: cut or commit, no limbo. Amazon is cutting more corporate retail jobs, Lululemon's new chief has overhauled the leadership team, and Apple has set October 13 for its smart home hub event. The advisors also want hard rules on SoundHound and Unusual Machines, and adding healthcare and international exposure is next on their list. Markets, it seems, are not the only things being rebalanced.
