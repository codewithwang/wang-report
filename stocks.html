Consumer names split. Her advisors have opened discussion on trimming that position gradually, in tranches over the coming weeks, with proceeds seeding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. Healthcare and international diversification are next on the list, alongside firm exit rules for the portfolio's more speculative names. Intel remains a decision deferred, and the advisors want it decided by their next review, not carried past it.
