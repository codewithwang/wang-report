By Charmaine LoNews AnchorFri, Sep 11

Fixed income sits quietly on the sidelines while the rest of the tape does the talking, with government paper anchoring the portfolio against whatever the growth names get up to. Rate mood remains the backdrop against which every technology and semiconductor decision gets made, and today is no exception. The broader indices, including the fund tracking the S&P 500, continue to serve as the ballast beneath a book tilted hard toward growth.

The technology sleeve remains the engine, and it is a crowded one, spanning chipmakers, software infrastructure, and a leveraged semiconductor position layered on top for good measure. Consumer names round out the cyclical exposure. The energy fund offers the one genuine hedge against everything else in the book moving together. A leveraged semiconductor ETF sitting inside a portfolio that already owns NVIDIA, AMD, Micron and a triple-levered chip bet is not diversification so much as a very strong opinion, stated three times.

NVIDIA is the name in focus, the largest position and now the subject of a trim, with her advisors discussing a staged reduction over several tranches in the coming weeks, proceeds earmarked to open a new Berkshire Hathaway stake. Looking further out, the advisors have flagged healthcare and international exposure as gaps to close, with hard rules under discussion for the more speculative names in the book. Intel is due a decision, one way or the other, rather than an indefinite hold.
