By Charmaine LoNews AnchorFri, Sep 4

Money left equities today, gold and Treasurys caught the bid, and the exodus showed up in fund flows out of the broad index products even as the mega-cap names that dominate them kept trading on their own scripts. Software took the worst of it, identity and infrastructure names swept lower in a sector-wide selloff that made no distinction between companies with a story and companies with an earnings miss. Semiconductors did their own thing entirely. Violently, in both directions.

The technology book is still the largest position by a wide margin, and its largest single name drew fresh attention as Nvidia put nearly thirteen billion dollars into Hugging Face, a sum that made headlines for its size and explained nothing about what a chip company wants with an AI hub. Adobe changed chief executives. Lululemon did not so much miss its outlook as renounce it, falling sharply on the news. Consumer cyclical and software infrastructure both had a rough day. The concentrated technology book, mercifully, did not move as one.

Her advisors have been trimming the Nvidia position in stages, reasoning that concentration is a risk you carry on purpose, not by accident. Proceeds are funding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. They are eyeing healthcare and international diversification for the weeks ahead, and drafting exit rules for the portfolio's smaller, more volatile names, the sort of discipline that only ever gets written down after the damage. Intel remains a decision deferred. The advisors are quick to note that deferring one is still making one.
