From the desk · Daily read
By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSat, Sep 26

Markets were closed or data wasn't reported today, so the tape itself is the story: no print on the S&P, the Nasdaq, or the Hang Seng, which leaves every desk quoting yesterday's close as if it still means something. Rate mood stays the same low hum it has been for weeks, nobody screaming, nobody popping champagne. When the numbers go quiet, the commentary gets louder, and today it got louder than usual.

The portfolio's own weather map splits along familiar lines. The technology book, still the largest position by a wide margin, carries the semiconductor and AI-adjacent names that move together on sentiment more than fundamentals lately. Consumer names ride on the same shopping and travel data everyone else is reading. The energy and index sleeves sit quietly in the background, doing what they're built to do, which is mostly nothing, and that is the point of owning them.

Her advisors have been talking about NVIDIA. The direction is to trim it down from its outsized share, in three tranches over the next month or so, with the first tranche funding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway. Healthcare and international diversification, through names like UNH, JNJ, and VXUS, are on the near-term list. Intel gets no more patience: cut or commit. On SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, the new house rule is blunt. No adds, trim on strength, out entirely if the thesis breaks.
