By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSun, Sep 13

Markets were closed for the data feed today, so the tape itself has nothing to report: no print on the S&P 500, the Nasdaq, or the Hang Seng. A portfolio built for growth still has to sit still on the days the market gives it nothing to react to.

Underneath that quiet, the sector mix tells the real story. This book leans hard into technology, with Apple, NVIDIA, Adobe, AMD, Intel, CrowdStrike, Palantir, SoundHound AI, Unusual Machines and Micron carrying a concentrated technology book, backstopped by broader index exposure through the Vanguard S&P 500 fund and amplified further by a leveraged semiconductor position. Consumer names in Amazon and Lululemon and a single software infrastructure holding in Okta round out the growth tilt. The Hong Kong Government iBond and the Vanguard Energy ETF sit apart as the ballast.

The advisors have been busy behind the scenes. Their current guidance calls for trimming NVIDIA down from its outsized perch, in three tranches over the next month or so, with part of the proceeds earmarked to open a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. Further out, they are eyeing a healthcare allocation through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, international exposure via VXUS, and firmer discipline on SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, alongside a verdict on Intel that is now overdue. A currency hedge and an estate tax review round out the watch list.
