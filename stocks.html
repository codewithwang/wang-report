By Charmaine LoNews AnchorThu, Jul 30

Markets are sitting still while the story underneath them is not. There is no fresh print on the S&P, the Nasdaq, or the Hang Seng today, which leaves the tape reading calmer than the news moving individual names.

Technology is doing the heavy lifting, and doing it unevenly. Adobe touched a two month high after the FTC fast tracked its Topaz Labs deal. Apple heads into Tim Cook's final earnings call with the stock at a record, a tidy bit of timing for an exit. NVIDIA, the largest position in the book, is drawing fire from an unlikely direction: Anthropic turning up competitive heat rather than a regulator or a short seller. AMD sank as SK Hynix results reignited fears the AI trade is unwinding. The semiconductor story now moves on any supplier's earnings, not just its own.

Consumer names split too. Amazon got its Zoox robotaxis cleared for driverless runs with no human controls, while Lululemon is fighting a class action over tariff refunds it is accused of keeping instead of passing to customers.

The advisors have been busiest around concentration. They are trimming NVIDIA down from its outsized perch in stages over the coming weeks, using the proceeds to open a new Berkshire Hathaway position.
