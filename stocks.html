Fixed income is quiet while the tape does the talking today, and the calm in rates is enough to keep risk appetite holding rather than retreating. The technology book, still the largest position by a wide margin, is the swing factor for the day. Semiconductors and the leveraged semiconductor exposure move hardest, geared as that sleeve is to the same names. Software infrastructure and the broader index holding ride along rather than lead, and energy does its own thing, detached from the tech tape entirely.

Inside the technology book, concentration risk is meeting a diversification instinct. The advisors have been discussing trimming NVIDIA down from its outsized share of the portfolio, in tranches over four to six weeks rather than in one move, with the first tranche funding a new position in Berkshire Hathaway Class B. The stake is described only as a stake.

Further out, the advisors have flagged building a healthcare allocation through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, adding international exposure through a broad ex-US fund, and writing explicit rules around SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines: no further adds, trim half on a double, exit in full if the thesis breaks. Intel remains a name the advisors say needs a decision, not a holding pattern, and a small Hong Kong dollar allocation is under discussion as a hedge against peg stress.

Even the tidiest portfolio still needs paperwork before it can relax.
