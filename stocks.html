By Charmaine LoNews AnchorTue, Sep 22

Markets opened quiet, the S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Hang Seng all lacking a clean print, leaving the tape read more in tone than in numbers. Rate mood stayed the dominant undercurrent: nothing moved, and everyone still called it a decision.

The portfolio's center of gravity remains technology, a concentrated book spanning chipmakers, software infrastructure, and AI-adjacent names, alongside a leveraged semiconductor position that amplifies whatever mood the sector is in. Consumer cyclical and a broad index holding round out the exposure. Fixed income, in the form of the HK government iBond, is the quiet ballast, and the energy sleeve, held via a diversified fund, trades as the counterweight to a growth-heavy book.

NVIDIA is the name in focus this morning. The advisors have discussed trimming it toward a smaller share of the portfolio, executed in tranches over the coming month and change, with proceeds seeding a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. Further out, they've flagged adding healthcare exposure, either UNH or JNJ, and building out international diversification through VXUS. On the smaller, higher-risk names, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, the guidance was blunt: no adds, trim on strength, exit if the thesis breaks. Intel remains a name to resolve, not to hold in limbo.
