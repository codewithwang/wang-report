By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSat, Sep 19

S&P 500, Nasdaq, and the Hang Seng all traded without a print worth citing today, which is its own kind of statement. Rate mood remains the dominant subplot for a portfolio this loaded with semiconductors, a 3X-leveraged sleeve among them, and the tape's silence has not slowed the advisors' appetite for restructuring.

Technology still dominates the mix here, concentrated and leveraged enough that a single name's move ripples through the leveraged semiconductor sleeve as much as the stock itself. Consumer cyclical names sit quieter by comparison. The fixed income sleeve, anchored by the HK government iBond, did what fixed income is supposed to do today, which is mostly nothing. Today that counted as a virtue.

NVIDIA is the name in focus. The advisors have discussed trimming it down from the largest position in the book, staged across several tranches over the coming weeks, with proceeds earmarked to open a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway Class B. Beyond that, they have flagged healthcare and international diversification as coming priorities. They want hard exit rules written for the portfolio's more speculative names before any of them double again. Intel and a possible HKD-linked hedge are under review too.

Nothing executed today. Just the paperwork getting done before the trading does.
