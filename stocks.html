From the desk · Daily read
By Charmaine LoNews AnchorThu, Sep 10

Markets were quiet on the print today. The major gauges offered little in the way of direction, and rate expectations held roughly where investors left them. That kind of stillness tends to read as calm. It is usually just the market taking a breath before deciding which way to lean, and today it declined to decide.

The sector story in this book is concentrated where the market's attention already lives. The largest position sits in a technology book anchored by chip and software names, with the leveraged semiconductor exposure amplifying whatever mood that group is in on a given session. Consumer names and the index fund track broad sentiment. The fixed income sleeve, the HK Government iBond, sits apart from all of it, doing the one thing bonds are still paid to do.

The advisors have been busy. They discussed trimming the NVIDIA position to a smaller share of the portfolio, selling in stages over the coming weeks rather than all at once, with part of the proceeds earmarked to open a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. Looking further out, they floated adding healthcare exposure through UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, along with international diversification via a broad ex-US fund. On the smaller, more speculative names, SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines, the advisors want hard rules in place before the next headline moves them.

Intel, meanwhile, has been flagged for a decision. Own it or don't. No more limbo.
