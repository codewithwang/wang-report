By Charmaine LoNews AnchorSun, Sep 6

Markets held a steady, cautious tone, the major benchmarks trading without fresh direction as investors wait on the next word from the rate-setters rather than act on the last one. Call it patience dressed up as a market, marking time.

Inside this book, the story is concentration meeting diversification. The technology sleeve remains the dominant force, still the largest position by a wide margin, carrying names from the established (Apple, NVIDIA, Adobe) to the speculative (SoundHound AI, Unusual Machines). Around it sit a broad index sleeve, an energy allocation, a leveraged semiconductor bet, and a single government bond. Four different bets, four different futures, sharing the same afternoon.

Her advisors have been busy with scissors and shopping lists. The plan on the table trims NVIDIA down from its outsized perch, in stages over the coming weeks, with the proceeds seeding a new position in Berkshire Hathaway. Momentum funding temperament, in other words. Waiting in the wings: a healthcare allocation, built around UnitedHealth or Johnson & Johnson, and an international fund to balance the home bias.

Intel is another matter. The advisors have run out of patience there, and a decision is coming either way. For SoundHound and Unusual Machines, they want hard exit rules written now, before the next swing writes the decision for them.
