From the desk · Daily read
By Charmaine LoNews AnchorThu, Sep 10

The tape reading is flat today. S&P 500, Nasdaq and Hang Seng aren't printing a level worth calling, and the honest read is that the macro backdrop is quiet, not decisive. Rate mood is still the dominant variable for any book leaning this heavily into semiconductors and software, and that dependency hasn't moved just because the indices haven't.

Technology and its leveraged shadow are doing the work here: a concentrated tech book paired with a triple leveraged semiconductor position that amplifies every move the chip names make, up or down. Consumer cyclical, software infrastructure and energy round out the picture as smaller counterweights. The fixed income sleeve, anchored by the HK government iBond, stays the quiet ballast while the tech names take the headlines.

NVIDIA is the largest position, and now the center of the conversation. The advisors have discussed trimming it toward a smaller share of the portfolio, selling in three tranches over four to six weeks, with proceeds from the first tranche funding a new stake in Berkshire Hathaway Class B. Further out, they've flagged adding healthcare exposure through UNH or JNJ and international diversification through VXUS, both built gradually.

They've also proposed hard rules for SoundHound AI and Unusual Machines: no further adds, half off on a double, full exit if the thesis breaks. And they want a decision on Intel. Not an extended holding pattern.
