A Mountain Kills, a Screen Settles, and Everyone Explains Themselves

A glacier let go on the Nepal-Tibet border and buried whole villages, while in boardrooms from Menlo Park to Ottawa the powerful spent the day explaining themselves to people who no longer believe the explanations.
Maarten Bakker · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Scientists now think it was ice snapping off a glacier, not just rain, that sent the wave through Nepal's border villages. Over a thousand people are still missing tonight. Meta agreed to pay up to eighteen billion dollars over claims its apps harm children, a settlement that reads less like contrition than like a company doing the math and finding remorse cheaper than the alternative. Ofgem raised the UK's energy price cap again. Iran and Oman reportedly struck a deal over shipping through Hormuz, oil dipped on the news, and Saudi Arabia is quietly arranging its forces near Yemen while that diplomacy plays out in one strait. China told its citizens to leave Eswatini, pressuring Taiwan's last African friend without firing a shot. Watch whether the Hormuz talk survives contact with Riyadh's war footing.
