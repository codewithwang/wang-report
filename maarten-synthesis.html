Iran and America Trade Fire Again, Nepal's Death Toll Keeps Climbing

Two crises escalating in parallel, one measured in missiles and oil prices, the other in a missing count that keeps revising upward, with Hong Kong reading both from a distance.
Maarten Bakker · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Iran fired on bases in Jordan after a fresh American strike, the Emiratis said they shot down a drone over their own waters, and oil went to ninety dollars a barrel. Traders are now pricing this crisis as real rather than contained. In Nepal, the missing count nearly doubled to over four thousand. The revision reflects how limited the initial information from flood-hit areas was. NASA sent a telescope up to map dark energy. Financial regulators spent Monday warning that AI is now the top risk to bank stability, a conclusion reached with the confidence of people who have just discovered something the rest of us assumed already. Institutional money piled into shipping stocks on the strength of the same Middle East tension rattling everyone else, because someone always finds the arbitrage in other people's disasters.
