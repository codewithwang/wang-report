Kushner Lands in Israel While a Minister Talks Body Counts

Gaza diplomacy and Gaza rhetoric arrived in Hong Kong's feed on the same afternoon, and the wire spent the day deciding which one was the real story.
Maarten Bakker · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Jared Kushner touched down in Israel today to do the thing sons-in-law of presidents apparently now do, which is meet an armed faction in Egypt and then walk into Netanyahu's office as though that were a normal Tuesday. It might actually work. Meanwhile Seoul got a different kind of news, Washington trimming military drills with the South as a gesture toward Pyongyang, the sort of goodwill offering that tends to look better in the press release than it does to the ally standing next to you. Anthropic's chief executive spent the day arguing the AI backlash is a crisis of trust rather than overhyped pessimism, a distinction companies reach for right before the trust problem gets worse. Private credit is flashing stress signals not seen since 2017, quietly. And in Congo, the Ebola outbreak just became the deadliest on record. Hong Kong closed the day watching oil hold steady on Gulf nerves nobody has resolved. Watch whether Kushner's Netanyahu meeting produces anything beyond a photograph, and whether Seoul says out loud what it clearly already thinks.
