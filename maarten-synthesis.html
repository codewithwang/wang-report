Dolly Parton Dies at 80; Washington Moves to Revoke Two Hundred Thousand Visas

A country legend's death, a mass visa revocation, and a Texas wildfire raced across the wire today.
Maarten Bakker · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Dolly Parton died at 80 after what her camp called a brief illness. Tributes came in from Elton John to Oprah Winfrey. Washington announced it would revoke up to 200,000 visas, calling it the largest such action in history. That is 200,000 individual Tuesdays getting ruined. Ontario's premier compared Washington's tariff posture to how China treats dissidents, a comparison that gets you uninvited to dinner but does tend to focus the mind. Texas watched a wildfire eat 50,000 acres in hours, and a French village lost 300 homes to a tornado. Weather does not read the news cycle. It interrupts it. Hong Kong's own export surge, up 53 percent on AI demand, barely registered against all that. Watch whether Canada's retaliatory tariffs, once announced, survive contact with actual Canadian exporters, and whether Texas gets the fire contained before the wind changes again.
