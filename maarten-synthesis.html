Kelce Calls Swift His Wife While Washington Explains a Catering Truck

A wedding, a $12.5 billion basketball sale, and a press secretary's exit competed for attention on a day the White House still owed everyone an explanation for a decoy plane.
Maarten Bakker · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Travis Kelce announced, in the comments section, that he now has a wife, which is either the least or most dramatic way anyone has confirmed a marriage to Taylor Swift, and either way it dominated more feeds today than anything out of Washington managed to. The White House pressed on with its own story, Karoline Leavitt stepping down as press secretary, timed such that nobody had finished asking about the catering truck before a new headline arrived to bury it. Meanwhile the actual money moved in Los Angeles, where Bob Iger and Josh Kushner agreed to buy the Lakers for $12.5 billion, a number that makes the franchise worth more than several small nations. Add an Illinois retiree suddenly a billion dollars richer, and a Texas helicopter crash that killed two soldiers grounding the day in something less cheerful, and you have Thursday: absurd, expensive, and occasionally tragic, often within the same news cycle. Watch whether Leavitt's replacement is named by next week, and whether the Lakers sale clears league ownership review without a hitch.
