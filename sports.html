What This Desk Believes

Asian capital is buying sport's plumbing (rights, leagues, venues, calendars), not its trophies; read the balance sheet, not the scoreboard, and the trophies follow within a decade.

The HKJC model (monopoly plus charity) is the template the Gulf funds are quietly studying; the first open imitation appears before the 2034 World Cup.
