One pick for every race from a fixed rule (official rating against the field, last three placings, rating change, the draw against course and distance, a fixed list of leading riders and yards, days since the last run, the going as HKJC states it, the Observatory's forecast); confidence is the gap to the second horse, held at 3 when the going is off or rain is likely; HK$200 a pick in confidence order until the week's HK$1,000 is used, the rest logged as passes and settled all the same; a Wednesday meeting takes at most HK$600 so the weekend is never left empty. What This Desk Believes

Asian capital is buying sport's plumbing (rights, leagues, venues, calendars), not its trophies; read the balance sheet, not the scoreboard, and the trophies follow within a decade.

The HKJC model (monopoly plus charity) is the template the Gulf funds are quietly studying; the first open imitation appears before the 2034 World Cup.
