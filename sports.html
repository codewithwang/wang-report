Klay Thompson Signs With Heat After Mavericks Buyout

Klay Thompson spent one season in Dallas averaging 14 points a night off a knee that never quite forgave the 2021 tear, and on Tuesday he left the way most Mavericks stories end lately: with a shot on the way out. The Mavericks bought him out, the Heat signed him hours later, and Thompson made sure Dallas knew exactly how the tenure read from his side before he'd even unpacked in Miami. Pat Riley now owns a fourth ring-chaser at the vet minimum, the same trick that turned Miami into a finals team on a Jimmy Butler trade discount two years running.

The Mavericks, for the record, are the same front office that shipped Luka Doncic to the Lakers in February 2025 and still hasn't fully answered for it in Dallas. Kyrie Irving getting cleared to return and Klay getting bought out in the same week is not a coincidence, it's a franchise doing inventory. Miami collects the buyout classes; Dallas keeps generating them. Nico Harrison built a roster in reverse, and the Heat just billed him for postage.
