NBA Free Agency Shuffle Sends Kuminga to Minnesota

Jonathan Kuminga is a Minnesota Timberwolf, which means the Warriors just finished a two-year negotiation by losing the argument to the player. Golden State spent the 2025 offseason trying to sign-and-trade him on their terms; Kuminga spent it reminding them he had other terms. He landed with a Timberwolves team one Anthony Edwards max extension away from being built entirely out of players who make opposing coaches sweat, and he did it without ever taking Golden State's version of the deal. For a franchise that has spent a decade telling restricted free agents what their leverage should be worth, watching a 23-year-old walk on his own valuation is the actual headline.

Golden State drafted Kuminga seventh in 2021 hoping he would grow into a third star next to Stephen Curry and Draymond Green. He never signed the extension that would have locked him into that plan at their price, and restricted free agency this summer became a staring contest the front office lost. Minnesota gets a 23-year-old former lottery pick on a roster still built around Edwards's below-market rookie extension, the same team-friendly math that let the Wolves outbid a bigger market without blinking. The Warriors, meanwhile, now have a Kuminga-sized hole in the frontcourt and a full offseason to explain why the plan changed twice.
