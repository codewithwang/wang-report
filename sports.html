Warriors Bring Draymond Green Back Under New Extension

Golden State re-signed Draymond Green this week, closing the door on the version of this offseason where the Warriors' longest-serving enforcer walks for nothing. Green, 36, has been the connective tissue of four championship rosters, the guy who guards centers, brings the ball up, and talks more trash per possession than anyone with a straight face should. Keeping him is not a basketball gamble. It is Golden State admitting the Stephen Curry championship window still has a number attached to it, and that number is smaller than it was in 2022.

Look at what surrounds the move. Golden State spent the same week fielding calls on Jonathan Kuminga's future and watching Klay Thompson trade speculation drift toward Miami, a franchise two years removed from trading Thompson outright. The Warriors are not rebuilding. They are triaging a core that won four titles in eight years and now has to be bought back one veteran-minimum-adjacent deal at a time. Green's new contract is the cheap part. The expensive part is the Curry extension clock that started the moment Golden State decided Green was worth keeping instead of flipping for young legs. That bill comes due before this roster gets another parade.
