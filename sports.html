Verstappen Turns Down Every Suitor, Signs Through 2030

Max Verstappen just took himself off a market that did not officially exist and made it disappear anyway. Red Bull confirmed Thursday the four-time champion has extended through 2030, ending a year of Mercedes and Aston Martin speculation that Verstappen himself never bothered to confirm or deny, which is its own kind of answer. He is 28. He will be 32 when this deal expires, which in Formula 1 driver years is roughly the same as signing a mortgage.

Here is what the extension actually protects: Red Bull's power-unit program, the one it spent hundreds of millions building from scratch with Ford after splitting from Honda, needed its only title-winning driver to not walk out the door mid-transition. A four-time champion bolting for Mercedes in year one of a new engine would have been the loudest vote of no confidence in the paddock. Verstappen didn't just sign a contract. He underwrote one.
