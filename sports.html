The 76ers Are Building Klay Thompson a Farewell Tour

The Klay Thompson rumor mill has landed on the Philadelphia 76ers, and the pitch is not basketball, it is theater. Scouts are already lowering expectations for what a 14th-season Thompson can give a rotation (Ira Winderman's reporting has one veteran scout warning teams not to expect much), but Philadelphia just spent the summer's biggest guarantee on a 36-year-old whose actual value now sits somewhere between "microwave scorer" and "traveling museum exhibit." That is not a scouting report. That is a business model.

Three splashes, one thread. Klay's market is priced on nostalgia, not efficiency. Dallas is fielding an "unexpected path" back to Luka Doncic before he has finished unpacking in Los Angeles, because a superstar's trade value never actually resets, it just goes into escrow. And the Mavericks' real return on the Doncic deal might turn out to be Tarik Biberovic, a throw-in nobody bothered to scout twice. None of these three stories is really about minutes per game. They are about franchises pricing a name over a season. Philadelphia is not signing a shooting guard. It is renting a highlight reel with a jersey number on it, and the box score was never going to be the point.
