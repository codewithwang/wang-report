Eddie Howe Is Out At Newcastle After Four Years

Newcastle United sacked Eddie Howe on Friday, ending a four-year run that took the club from relegation fringe to a Champions League return and, in March, the League Cup, the club's first major trophy since 1969. The decision lands three weeks before the Premier League season opens, with Newcastle already tracking Bruno Guimaraes as their marquee summer swing and now doing it while also interviewing his next manager. Arsenal, notably, are circling that same Guimaraes deal, which means the Saudi Public Investment Fund's club is about to negotiate with a rival suitor over its best midfielder while running a beauty pageant for a new head coach in the same news cycle.

That is the actual cost of the timing. PIF has spent five years underwriting Newcastle's rise, and the football returned on that investment, the trophy, the European nights, the league finishes climbing every year Howe had the job. What it has not yet returned is a manager PIF trusts enough to survive a mid-table stretch, because Howe delivered results and still lost the job anyway. The next appointment inherits a Champions League squad, a Guimaraes negotiation already live, and owners who just proved that even a trophy is not job security at St James' Park.
