LIV’s unpaid suppliers, Musk’s costly election regrets

Plus the Crimea bridge diplomatic dispute and four arrests in an international ransomware investigation.

Evening briefing

  • SportsLIV paid golfers while suppliers waited

    BBC Sport’s review of invoices and court filings found UK supplier bills already overdue when LIV paid golfers $34.2 million between 17 August and 3 September, before seeking bankruptcy protection on 8 September. Only $2 million has so far been earmarked for unsecured creditors, whose 30 largest claims exceed $64 million; LIV did not respond, and suppliers still cannot count on being paid.

  • LifeMusk rues nearly $500 million in election spending

    Variety reports that Elon Musk told the DealBook Summit on 18 November he felt “pretty foolish” after spending nearly $500 million backing Republicans in midterms that gave Democrats both chambers of Congress. He suggested the losses might have been worse without his spending, leaving half a billion dollars with the consolation prize of a counterfactual.

  • WorldRussia challenges Netherlands over Crimea bridge strike

    The Guardian reports that Russia’s embassy questioned whether the Netherlands had joined the war after Dutch-based Destinus said its missiles were used in Ukraine’s 17 November attack on the Kerch bridge connecting Russia and annexed Crimea. The manufacturer’s statement does not establish Dutch government participation; a further attack on 18 November temporarily halted bridge traffic, with damage still being assessed.

  • CyberFour detained in international ransomware investigation

    BleepingComputer reports that Ukrainian authorities, working with the FBI, detained four suspected members of a ransomware organisation that police link to more than $40.5 million in payments from at least 28 companies. Investigators say attackers impersonated IT support to obtain corporate access; the suspects remain unconvicted, and the payment total represents neither recovered funds nor the full damage, which police are still establishing.
