A short evening edition

  • FinanceTech sell-off spreads to Asia

    Japan’s Nikkei and South Korea’s Kospi closed down 6% on Friday after Thursday’s 5.5% Nasdaq fall, with the BBC reporting investor anxiety over AI spending returns and political opposition to data centres.   • Hong KongExchange Fund quarterly earnings fall 74%

    Hong Kong’s Exchange Fund earned HK$18.6 billion in the third quarter, down from HK$70.8 billion a year earlier, according to SCMP’s account of Friday’s HKMA announcement. Chief executive Eddie Yue warned that sharp market swings and geopolitical risks could make the final quarter more challenging.

  • WorldBeijing’s cross-border corruption law takes immediate effect

    China’s legislature passed an Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law on Friday, establishing mechanisms for international cooperation and offshore asset recovery, SCMP reports. Its reach includes investigating Chinese firms’ misconduct overseas while blocking unauthorised foreign investigations in China, giving cross-border enforcement a distinctly directional understanding of the border.
