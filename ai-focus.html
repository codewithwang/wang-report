One Paper, Explained
Oct 18
Equal CER, Unequal Damage: Severity-Weighted Character Error Rate for OCR
Nguyen Nguyen, Yongyi Zang

Nguyen Nguyen and Yongyi Zang propose measuring OCR errors by their severity. Their claim: two systems with the same character error rate can cause unequal damage. The mechanism is to give consequential character mistakes more weight. Misreading a digit in a payment amount and misspelling a descriptive word should contribute differently to the score. For a team building document workflows, this changes the model selection criterion. Evaluate which errors survive into business decisions, then use that measure to compare OCR systems and decide what needs review. A lower error count alone does not establish a better document pipeline.

One paper a week that changes how people build with models, chosen and explained by Aya Nakamura: what it shows, the mechanism in one step, and what it changes for a team building on models.
