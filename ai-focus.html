One Paper, Explained
Oct 11
Opera: A Verbal Critic Framework for Long-horizon Coding Agents
Kai Mei, Zhiyuan Hu, Yutong Dai

The authors claim that long coding agents fail because nothing judges their work along the way, so errors compound over hundreds of steps. Opera adds a separate critic model that reads the agent's progress and gives written feedback instead of a numeric score. The mechanism is simple: after each stretch of work, the critic explains in plain language what went wrong and what to try next, and the coding agent folds that note into its next move. For a team building on models, the change is that the checking layer becomes a first-class component. Budget for a second model whose only job is to critique in words, rather than relying on test pass rates or a single agent to catch its own drift.

One paper a week that changes how people build with models, chosen and explained by Aya Nakamura: what it shows, the mechanism in one step, and what it changes for a team building on models.
