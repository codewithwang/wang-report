Yuen Long Dog Kills Two Pets, Nobody Owns The Problem

A 60-kilogram brown dog went after two small pets at Yoho in Yuen Long on August 17, killing both in about 20 minutes before anyone could stop it. Turns out the same dog bit a corgi last month. So this wasn't a surprise to anyone who lives near it. Someone knew. Nobody acted.

That's the actual story here, not the dog. Hong Kong has rules about dangerous dogs on paper, but no one seems to own the job of following up after the first bite. A corgi gets bitten in July, everyone moves on, and by August two more pets are dead in a shopping mall car park. If you own a small dog in Yuen Long right now, you're the one checking over your shoulder, not the department that had a month's warning.
