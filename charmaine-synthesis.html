A Day Where Iran Escalated and Nepal Kept Counting Its Dead

Fresh strikes on Iran and a Himalayan death toll that passed one thousand overnight. Hong Kong still had a stock debut and a soccer retirement to talk about over lunch.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Good morning. The Strait of Hormuz has now had actual tankers struck by actual projectiles. Nepal's death toll crossed one thousand overnight, and the Nepal-Tibet flood count keeps widening faster than anyone can confirm it. In between, Shein listed in Hong Kong on Tuesday, shares sliding on debut after a years long search for a market willing to have it. Washington has vowed to hit Iran hard after the first direct exchange of strikes in weeks, and bond yields climbed to levels unseen since 2008. Lionel Messi announced his retirement from Argentina's national team, tidy news in an untidy week. SCOTUS greenlit the White House ballroom over the chief justice's own dissent. Watch whether Hormuz stays a shipping story or becomes an oil price story by tonight.
