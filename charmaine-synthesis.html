A Landslide in South Korea and a Landslide Win in Zambia

A day of results and reckonings, from a president re-elected in Lusaka to an actor gone far too soon, with Hong Kong's own housing wait still the quieter story worth watching.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Zambia's Hakainde Hichilema won a second term outright, a clean electoral result that makes headlines precisely because it is no longer the norm on that continent, or several others. Brazil's Lula announced a run at a fourth term with Bolsonaro's son standing between him and it, proof that dynasty politics needs no dynasty, just a surname. In South Korea, nearly a metre of rain fell on one city and triggered a landslide. Deaths have been confirmed. Hollywood lost Hayden Panettiere far too young. Meta's child-safety trial opened in the United States, with several states now demanding real changes to Instagram and Facebook rather than another round of promises. DR Congo's Ebola outbreak was confirmed the deadliest in the country's history, a grim milestone. Closer to home, expect more scrutiny of how Hong Kong's own housing timelines compare to the "good news" framing officials gave them this month. Watch whether Zambia's opposition accepts the count and whether Seoul's rain lets up before the weekend.
