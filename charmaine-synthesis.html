A Ballroom Gets Blocked While Bangkok Buries Its Children

A federal court halts a $400 million White House renovation on the same day a school shooting outside Bangkok leaves eight dead, and Hong Kong watches Typhoon Dolphin bear down on the coast it just cleared skies over.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Good morning. The day's clearest story is also its saddest: a school shooting outside Bangkok has left eight dead, including the suspected attacker. There is no clever line for that, only the fact of it. Closer to home, Typhoon Dolphin is bearing down on eastern China after knocking out power to 44,000 buildings in Okinawa, so umbrellas will do very little again this weekend. In Washington, an appeals court halted construction on Donald Trump's $400 million White House ballroom, a project that survived budget scrutiny longer than most renovations survive a Hong Kong flat inspection. A federal judge fined Meta $567 million over how young people use its platforms, the largest such penalty yet. Fines keep getting bigger while the apps keep looking the same. Newcastle sold Bruno Guimaraes to Arsenal for 75 million pounds, a tidy number that says less about the midfielder than about PIF's own accounting elsewhere. IBM published quantum circuits that more than thirty outside teams have tried and failed to break. That is either reassuring or exactly what a quantum computer would want you to think. Watch Hormuz, watch the sky.
