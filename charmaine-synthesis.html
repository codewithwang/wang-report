Kelce and Swift Say Vows While Washington Sends Another Carrier

A wedding at Madison Square Garden, a solar eclipse over Cornwall, and a fresh US aircraft carrier headed to the Gulf all landed on the same news day, and only one of them was actually a surprise.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Good morning. Travis Kelce called Taylor Swift his wife for the first time today, and the internet, for once, agreed on something: the wedding was lovely and Madison Square Garden has never hosted a more expensive toast. Cristiano Ronaldo also married, more quietly, which suggests August is simply the month for it. Overhead, a near total solar eclipse swept Cornwall and parts of Spain. Washington's news cycle was less romantic. The White House confirmed Karoline Leavitt is stepping down as press secretary, and a fresh aircraft carrier is heading to the Middle East as Iran strain builds, which the administration will describe as prudence and everyone else will describe as a pattern. Meanwhile lawmakers are demanding answers after reports that sailors aboard the USS Abraham Lincoln tried to jump overboard during an extended deployment. Closer to the wallet, the US sold 30 year bonds at the highest borrowing cost since 2001. Watch whether Washington says anything real about the Lincoln today, and whether that carrier order gets an explanation beyond routine.
