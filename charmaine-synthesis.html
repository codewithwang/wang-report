Dolly Parton Dies at 80; Hong Kong Gets a Date for Its Big Plan

Nashville is mourning the artist who wrote the songs and owned the masters herself. Hong Kong got a September 16 date for its first five-year plan, and Ottawa and Washington kept refusing to blink.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Good morning. Dolly Parton died in Nashville at 80 after a brief cancer illness. Tributes came in from Elton John to Oprah Winfrey. She wrote her own songs, owned her own masters, and built an independent business over six decades in an industry that was often slow to credit her.

John Lee has set September 16 for Hong Kong's first five-year plan and policy address, giving the city three weeks to speculate about what is in it and the government three weeks to make sure the document says something. Hong Kong's exports did not wait for a five-year plan: they surged 53.4 percent in June on AI-linked demand, the fastest monthly growth since 1984. Nvidia's own quarter explains some of that appetite, with revenue more than doubling on AI demand. Canada and Washington are still not speaking calmly. Ottawa is readying retaliatory tariffs, and Doug Ford compared the treatment to Communist China. Watch whether the retaliation lands as promised.
