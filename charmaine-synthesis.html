A By-Election Landslide and a Guilty Plea Bookend a Loud Week

Farage wins the seat he engineered, Luigi Mangione ends his federal case with a plea, and Hong Kong's own August closes out under the same heat warnings as everyone else's.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Good morning. Nigel Farage won the Clacton by-election he called on himself, taking 63 percent against a man dressed as a rocket ship, who came second with a personal best. In New York, Luigi Mangione is expected to plead guilty in the federal case over the UnitedHealthcare shooting, closing one chapter of a story that turned a killing into a folk cause. Manchester's mayor is calling Britain a tinderbox after a week of extreme heat, and the FT reports a second UK train derailment in under 24 hours. DR Congo's Ebola outbreak has reached a sixth province, the fastest spread on record, and a tunnel collapse at an Indian hydropower project has killed at least seven workers. Washington is fielding questions about conditions aboard the carrier Lincoln, deployed nine months and counting, while insisting the ship is fine. Watch whether Britain's rail safety board says anything by evening, and whether Congo's outbreak numbers hold through the weekend.
