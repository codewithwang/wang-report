A Governor Recruits a Republican Just to Keep Her Job

Michigan hands progressives a Senate primary win, Arizona hands its governor's race a bipartisan running mate, and Hong Kong wakes to a Moon crash nobody meant to happen.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Good morning. Katie Hobbs, Arizona's Democratic governor, is reportedly set to name a Republican, John Giles, as her running mate for November. Michigan gave its progressives a cleaner win, with Abdul El-Sayed taking the Democratic Senate primary and becoming the header on every strategist's memo about what the party's base wants. Science handed the morning something gentler: astronomers are treating an accidental SpaceX rocket crash into the Moon as a data windfall. In Los Angeles, officials named the cause of last year's Eaton fire, an inactive transmission tower belonging to Southern California Edison, closing one question and opening several liability ones. Kyiv had a harder night. Seventeen dead in Russian strikes as Ukraine's interceptor stock runs thin. And at least twelve American states are fending off cyberattacks on their water systems.
