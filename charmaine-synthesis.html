A Ferry Sinks Off Cyprus While Iceland Says No Thank You

A capsized ferry, a rejected referendum, and a robot cholesterol drug that is still working a year later carry more of today than the oil deal everyone will talk about instead.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Good morning. Off northern Cyprus a ferry capsized overnight. At least eight people are dead and more than twenty are still missing. Iceland, asked plainly whether to reopen EU membership talks, said no, 52.8 percent of it anyway, a country that likes its fish quotas the way they are. In science, CRISPR Therapeutics' one-time liver edit is still holding down cholesterol and triglycerides a full year after infusion. The company will keep watching these same patients for years yet, so treat this as the trailer, not the film. Hollywood had its own kind of staying power: Spider-Man: Brand New Day posted the biggest domestic opening in box office history the same week China let the franchise back into its cinemas after seven dark years, a sign of which negotiator actually won. In sport, UFC bought a knockout in Shanghai for one hundred thousand dollars the same week Saudi Arabia's six billion dollar golf experiment formally closed, proof that sometimes the cheap seat is the good seat. Watch the Cyprus search numbers through the day.
