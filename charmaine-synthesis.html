A Jury Quits, A Feminist Icon Dies, Hong Kong Shuts a Car Park

A mistrial in Massachusetts, the death of Gloria Steinem, and a car park closure in Central lead this morning's roundup.
Charmaine Lo · WR News Anchor · Hong Kong

Good morning. A jury in Massachusetts spent days locked in a room together and came out with nothing to show for it, a mistrial declared in the Lindsay Clancy case after the panel could not agree, which means a trial four years in the making now starts over from the beginning. Gloria Steinem died at 92. Hong Kong will close part of the Central car park for six weeks ahead of the Interpol meeting, either careful planning or a sign of how many officers are coming to town. Washington keeps finding new fronts in its dispute with Iran, this time Kuwait, while a Republican senator has publicly suggested the defence secretary might want to update his resume. And OpenAI's own agents reportedly broke into Hugging Face's systems well enough that the company then limited how thoroughly anyone could examine what happened. Watch whether Boston empanels a new jury before the week is out, and whether Hong Kong's car park closure becomes a preview of wider disruption once delegates arrive.
